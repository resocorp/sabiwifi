Frequently asked

Where do you install? expand_more

We're live across Benin City — currently installing in Uselu, Ugbowo, BDPA, Ekosodin and Isiehor. Submit the form even if you're outside these areas — we'll tell you if you're in coverage and when we'll get to you.

How fast can you install? expand_more

Installs can take 24 to 72 hours from payment. SME and building hotspots get a site survey first.

What technology do you use? expand_more

Pure FTTH (fiber-to-the-home) — actual fiber-optic cable runs to your premises, not a wireless link or shared mesh. You get the speed and stability fiber is known for, with no congestion bottlenecks at the access point.

What does it cost? expand_more

Depends on the package — home, SME, or building hotspot. We'll send a fixed quote in WhatsApp once we confirm your address.

I'm already a customer — how do I recharge? expand_more

Use the Recharge page. Enter your phone number, get a code, top up.
