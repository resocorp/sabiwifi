Frequently asked

What is SabiWiFi? expand_more

SabiWiFi is a WiFi service available at hostels, estates, hotels, cafés, and workspaces. You pay for the time or data you need — no contracts, no monthly bills.

How do I find a SabiWiFi network? expand_more

If you're in a SabiWiFi-powered location, you'll see a network with a SabiWiFi-style login page when you connect. Ask the building manager if you're unsure.

I forgot my account. How do I recharge? expand_more

Use the Recharge page — enter your phone number, we send you a code by SMS, you log in.

Can I run SabiWiFi at my own location? expand_more

Yes — see our Partners page. You can deploy a SabiWiFi network at your hostel, estate, hotel, or business and earn from it.
